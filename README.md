## No permissions, ever

The page must never ask for a permission, on phone or desktop. No motion or orientation access, no location, no notifications, no camera or microphone, nothing else a browser would ask the visitor to allow.

It runs on touching, dragging and clicking, the wheel and the keyboard, and what the browser gives without asking. A feature that would need permission does not go in.

## One screen, and a peach to turn

The page does not scroll. Take hold of the peach, or of anything around it, and turn it as you would a model in the hand: left and right spin it, up and down tip it, and a flick keeps it going. The story turns with it, a scene to each half turn and one scene to a hold. A tap is a bite. The wheel and the arrow keys turn it too.
